Core Competencies

Strategic and technical capabilities supporting industrial transformation and operational excellence:

Industrial Modernization & Operational Improvement

Assessment of industrial and commercial operations to identify opportunities for technology adoption, systems optimization, infrastructure efficiency, and resource productivity improvement. Identification of energy efficiency, electrification, and decarbonization opportunities across industrial supply chains including manufacturing, food and beverage, pharmaceutical, and chemical sectors. Development of strategies that enhance manufacturing performance, operational resilience, competitiveness, and long-term sustainable economic development.

Strategic Advisory & Program Delivery

Strategic and technical advisory to regulated utilities and C&I organizations on energy efficiency, sustainability, and operational improvement initiatives. Design, implementation, evaluation, and continuous improvement of Demand Side Management programs, driving customer engagement and measurable energy, cost, and emissions reductions.

Capital Investment Evaluation & Technical Due Diligence

Technical, financial, and risk evaluation of major capital investment proposals. Development of investment assessments, business cases, and evidence-based recommendations supporting responsible allocation of public and organizational resources, including lifecycle cost analysis, financial modelling, and investment decision support.

Project Performance Evaluation & Measurement and Verification

Monitoring project implementation, evaluation of project performance outcomes, and development of improvement recommendations. Application of M&V methodologies, data analysis, performance reporting, and accountability frameworks supporting continuous improvement and informed decision-making.

Stakeholder Engagement & Partnership Management

Collaboration with government agencies, utilities, engineering consultants, contractors, technology providers, and private sector stakeholders. Cross-functional coordination of multidisciplinary teams and facilitation of technical workshops, stakeholder engagements, and knowledge transfer initiatives.

Building Performance, Simulation Modelling & Engineering Analysis

Assessment and modelling of commercial and industrial building energy systems using simulation tools, engineering calculations, billing data analysis, and field measurements. Evaluation of HVAC, building envelope, lighting, controls, motors and drives, weatherization, electrification, and renewable energy measures to quantify energy use, demand savings, and operational performance outcomes against established baselines.

Program Impact Evaluation & Cost-Effectiveness

Evaluation of utility energy efficiency, electrification, and load management programs through impact, process, and market evaluation. Quantification of verified energy and demand savings against code, standard practice, and custom baselines. Assessment of free ridership, spillover, and net-to-gross ratios to determine net program impacts. Benefit-cost analysis using Total Resource Cost, Utility Cost Test, Participant Test, and Ratepayer Impact Measure frameworks to determine program cost-effectiveness and support program (re)design, investment decisions, and regulatory reporting.
